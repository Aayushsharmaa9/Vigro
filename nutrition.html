Nutrition

Simple, balanced eating — no extreme diets, no restriction.

🍽️ Balanced Meal Ideas

Aim for a plate that's roughly one part protein, one part carbs, and a good portion of vegetables — eggs with roti and vegetables, dal-rice with curd, or paneer with a salad all work well.

🥚 Protein-Rich Foods

Eggs Milk / Curd Dal Paneer Soy / Tofu Chicken / Fish Peanuts

🥦 Fruits & Vegetables

Try to include a fruit and a vegetable at most meals — they support recovery, digestion, and everyday energy. Variety matters more than any single "superfood."

💧 Hydration

Drink water steadily through the day rather than forcing a fixed target. On workout days, sip a bit more before and after training.

⏱️ Pre / Post-Workout Food

Before: a light snack with carbs, like a banana or toast, an hour or so beforehand.

After: a normal meal with protein and carbs within a couple of hours — like eggs and toast, or dal-rice.

📘 Nutrition Basics

Food is fuel for growth, focus, and recovery — especially during teenage years. This isn't about cutting calories or chasing a number on a scale. Eat regular, balanced meals and let consistency do the work.
