Recovery

Growth happens between workouts, not just during them.

😴 Sleep

Aim for 8–9 hours whenever possible, especially on training days. Sleep is when most muscle repair and growth actually happens.

🧘 Rest Days

Rest days aren't wasted days — they let your body absorb the training. Skipping them consistently leads to burnout, not faster progress.

🤸 Stretching & Mobility

A few minutes of stretching after a workout, or a dedicated mobility session, keeps joints healthy and movement quality high over time.

Start Mobility Flow

💡 Recovery Tips

  • Light walking on rest days keeps blood flowing without adding fatigue.
  • Eating enough protein supports muscle repair.
  • Consistent sleep and wake times help recovery more than any supplement.

🩹 Soreness Guidance

Mild muscle soreness a day or two after training is normal. Sharp pain, joint pain, or soreness that gets worse over time is not — scale back and consider resting that area longer.
